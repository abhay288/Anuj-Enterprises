## 💰 Database Upgrade & Cost Policy

1. **Free-Tier Usage:** The platform currently utilizes the MongoDB Atlas cluster (`anujenterprises.nix5vcy.mongodb.net`).
2. **Growth Threshold Monitoring:** Storage and connection limits are audited monthly during regular maintenance.
3. **Upgrade Protocol:** If database usage approaches 80% of cluster capacity, Qyvero Technologies will notify Anuj Enterprises with a formal usage breakdown and recommended dedicated tier (e.g. M10).
4. **Zero Silent Charges:** Qyvero Technologies will **NEVER** incur paid database or third-party infrastructure expenses without explicit written authorization from Anuj Enterprises.
